Your privacy is our top priority. This policy explains how Poka CE handles your data.

## 1. Local Data Storage Only
Poka CE operates entirely offline. All of your financial data, transactions, and settings are stored locally on your device using an internal SQLite database. We do not transmit, sync, or back up your data to any external servers, clouds, or third parties.

## 2. No Accounts or Identity Required
You do not need to create an account, provide an email address, or verify your identity to use Poka CE. We do not collect or store any personal identifiable information (PII).

## 3. No Analytics, Tracking, or Telemetry
We do not use any third-party analytics, crash reporting tools, telemetry, or trackers. Your usage patterns, financial inputs, and app interactions remain completely private and unknown to the creators, contributors, and any third party.

## 4. Device Permissions
- **Biometrics:** Used strictly for the app lock feature if you choose to enable it. This is handled entirely by your device's native OS, and Poka CE never accesses your raw biometric data.
- **Storage:** Only requested when you actively choose to export or import your database backup file.

## 5. Third-Party Links
Poka CE may contain standard web links to our official GitHub repository. We do not track your clicks or inject any tracking parameters into these links.

## 6. Your Responsibility
Because Poka CE is fully offline and does not sync to the cloud, **you are entirely responsible for backing up your own data**. If you lose your device, uninstall the app without a manual backup, or clear the app data, your data cannot be recovered by the creators or contributors of Poka CE.
